A platform for managing **financial BPO** operations. It centralizes accounts payable and receivable, reconciliation and operational reports in a single flow.

## Highlights

- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Financial reconciliation and audit trail
- Consolidated operational reports
- CI/CD pipeline with containerized deploys
