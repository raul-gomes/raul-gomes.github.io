Co-founder and developer of a financial management platform for professionals who have both a personal (CPF) and a business (CNPJ) tax ID. Personal and business budgets are shown separately by category and subcategory, with short- and medium-term goals and spending limits per category.

## Highlights

- RESTful FastAPI APIs and PostgreSQL modeling optimized for queries by category and period (daily, monthly, yearly)
- React dashboard with charts for income sources and spending versus limits
- Docker Compose containerization and CI/CD
- Product definition and brand positioning
